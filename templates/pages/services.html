Who We Care For

Hands-on Health delivers compassionate home-based care customized to the specific medical condition and daily life requirements of:

  • Bedridden patients
  • Surgical & post-op recovery
  • Hospital discharge recovery
  • Chronic condition management
  • Daily living activity support
  • Palliative & supportive care
  • Short-term home assistance
  • Long-term home nursing
  • Families seeking caregiver relief
