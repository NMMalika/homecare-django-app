Why Choose Hands-On Health

Premium Home Healthcare & Private Nursing in Namibia

Compassionate, clinical excellence delivered directly to your home.

Hands-On Health offers comprehensive, individualized home care services designed to uphold patient dignity while supporting independent living. From skilled clinical nursing and post-operative recovery to long-term palliative and elderly care, our qualified medical professionals ensure your loved ones receive personalized support across Windhoek and nationwide.

Skilled & Compassionate Practitioners

Our registered nurses and certified caregivers provide complete clinical oversight, from medication management and wound care to vital monitoring and mobility support.

Professional Approach: We provide structured home-based support according to individual patient needs.
Care Where You Live: Patients receive appropriate support without leaving the comfort of their own home.
Individualised Care: Care arrangements are tailored to the patient's condition and specific requirements.
Family Support: We work alongside families to make caring for a loved one more manageable.
Compassionate Service: We combine professional clinical care with genuine human compassion.
Flexible Care Arrangements: Customized schedules including short-term, long-term, day, overnight, and stay-in care.
